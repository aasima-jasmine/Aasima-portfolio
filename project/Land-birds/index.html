Avian Habitats & Traits

Adaptations allowing birds to flourish across regions

Land Birds

Reside primarily on ground levels, open grasslands, gardens, and cityscapes. Adaptable for terrestrial movement and foraging.

Examples: Sparrow, Pigeon, Crow

Forest Birds

Occupy tree canopies, dense woodlands, and equatorial jungles. Specialized for vertical climbing, branch grasping, and foliage navigation.

Examples: Parrot, Woodpecker, Owl

Water Birds

Stationed along riverbanks, inland wetlands, coastal lagoons, and open waters. Engineered for buoyant swimming and diving.

Examples: Duck, Swan, Kingfisher
Key Takeaway: Beak shape, wing structure, and foot anatomy directly correspond to dietary and habitat requirements.
