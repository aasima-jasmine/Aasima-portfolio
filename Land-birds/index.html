Avian Habitats & Traits

Field notes and behavioral adaptations

Land Birds

Ground & City

Live on the ground, in gardens, and throughout metropolitan areas. Prominent examples include sparrow, pigeon, crow, and mynah.

Diet: Grains, small ground insects, and dropped fruits.

Forest Birds

Dense Jungle

Inhabit tree canopies and dense jungles. Characteristic species include parrot, woodpecker, owl, and peacock.

Nesting: Secure high branch nests; feed on tree fruit and bark insects.

Water Birds

Rivers & Seas

Stationed along riverbanks, inland lakes, and coastlines. Includes duck, swan, flamingo, and kingfisher.

Physiology: Long stilt legs and specialized beaks designed to fish and swim.

Birds thrive across diverse ecosystems, each exhibiting specialized morphological traits for survival.
